FAQ

Questions clients ask

What does Grid and Glint do?

Grid and Glint is a brand, web and AI studio. We design brand identities, design and build Webflow websites, create 3D and motion for the web, and integrate AI chatbots and workflows into the tools companies already use.

How long does a website project take?

Most marketing websites take 6 to 10 weeks from kickoff to launch. A brand identity takes 4 to 6 weeks. A combined identity and website project usually runs about 12 weeks.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is priced on a fixed written scope. Send a short brief and we reply within two working days with a price range, a timeline and what is included.

Why do you build websites in Webflow?

Webflow gives your team a visual CMS to update pages without a developer, produces fast, clean pages that search engines read well, and hosts securely. We also build custom code when a project needs it.

Are your websites optimised for search and AI answers?

Yes. Every site launches with a clear heading structure, page titles and descriptions, structured data, fast load times, accessible markup and content written to answer real customer questions directly.

What kind of AI integration do you build?

We build website chatbots and assistants, lead and brief intake, content workflows that draft into your Webflow CMS for review, and internal knowledge assistants that answer from your own documents.

Is an AI chatbot safe for my customers and my data?

We set up assistants to say they are assistants, to hand difficult questions to a person, and to answer only from content you approve. Your data stays in your own accounts, and a person reviews anything sent to customers or published.

Do we own the work when the project ends?

Yes. You own the design files, the Webflow site, your domain, your AI accounts and all data from day one. We hand over everything with documentation and training.
