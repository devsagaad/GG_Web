FAQ

Questions clients ask

What does Grid and Glint do?

Grid and Glint is a brand, web and AI studio. We design brand identities, including the motion and 3D that bring a mark to life, design and build Webflow websites, and build Custom Systems that run on your own premises: booking, staff records, site assistants and lead triage.

How long does a website project take?

Most marketing websites take 6 to 10 weeks from kickoff to launch. A brand identity takes 4 to 6 weeks. A combined identity and website project usually runs about 12 weeks.

How much does it cost?

Brand identity starts at ₱45,000, a Webflow website at ₱65,000, and both together (Launch) at ₱95,000. Custom Systems start at ₱350,000 plus the server, and Full Build at ₱445,000 plus the server. Your exact price is in the written proposal we send within 48 hours of our first call.

Why do you build websites in Webflow?

Webflow gives your team a visual CMS to update pages without a developer, produces fast, clean pages that search engines read well, and hosts securely. We also build custom code when a project needs it.

Are your websites optimised for search and AI answers?

Yes. Every site launches with a clear heading structure, page titles and descriptions, structured data, fast load times, accessible markup and content written to answer real customer questions directly.

What are Custom Systems?

Custom Systems: one system built around how your business runs, owned by you and running on your own server. Examples are booking and reminders, staff records, a site assistant and lead triage. They start at ₱350,000 plus the server.

Is the AI safe to put in front of my customers?

It always says it's an assistant, answers only from content you've approved, and hands hard questions to a person. Nothing goes to a customer or gets published without a person approving it. We test it before launch and keep testing it after.

Do your systems need AI?

No. AI is optional. If a system uses it, it calls an outside AI provider you approve; it never runs on your server, and we tell you exactly what leaves your premises. If you would rather nothing leaves, we build it without AI.

Do we own the work when the project ends?

Yes. Once the final payment clears, you own the design files, the Webflow site, your content, your data and the systems we build for you, including the code. Your domain and your Webflow hosting stay in your name throughout. We hand over everything with documentation and training.
