FAQ

Questions clients ask

What does Grid and Glint do?

Grid and Glint is a brand, web and systems studio. We design brand identities, including the motion and 3D that bring a mark to life, design and build Webflow websites, and build software and AI into how businesses run: booking, guest experiences, site assistants and lead triage.

How long does a website project take?

Most marketing websites take 6 to 10 weeks from kickoff to launch. A brand identity takes 4 to 6 weeks. A combined identity and website project usually runs about 12 weeks.

How much does it cost?

Every project is scoped to what you need, so there is no price list. Our assistant asks a few questions about your project and finds a time that works; then you meet the team, and a written proposal follows.

Why do you build websites in Webflow?

Webflow gives your team a visual CMS to update pages without a developer, produces fast, clean pages that search engines read well, and hosts securely. We also build custom code when a project needs it.

Are your websites optimised for search and AI answers?

Yes. Every site launches with a clear heading structure, page titles and descriptions, structured data, fast load times, accessible markup and content written to answer real customer questions directly.

What systems do you build?

Systems that take work off your plate: RSVP and shared galleries, booking and reminders, site assistants, lead and inbox triage, and custom builds we scope with you.

Is the AI safe to put in front of my customers?

It always says it's an assistant, answers only from content you've approved, and hands hard questions to a person. Nothing goes to a customer or gets published without a person approving it. We test it before launch and keep testing it after.

Do I need my own AI account?

No. By default your systems run on AI we manage for you: we choose the models, keep them up to date and switch providers if one goes down. If you'd rather use your own AI account, we can connect that instead.

Do we own the work when the project ends?

Yes. You own the design files, the Webflow site, your domain, your content, your data and the systems we build for you. When we manage the AI, we hold the provider accounts and you get your own key and usage dashboard. We hand over everything with documentation and training.
