FAQ

Before you ask

What kind of AI integration do you build?

Customer chatbots and assistants, workflow automation, internal knowledge assistants that answer from your own documents, and agents that take action such as qualifying leads or booking appointments, all with a person in the loop.

Is an AI chatbot safe for my customers and my data?

We set up assistants to say they are assistants, to hand difficult questions to a person, and to answer only from content you approve. Your data stays in your own accounts, and a person reviews anything sent to customers or published.

How long does an AI integration take?

Most AI integration projects take 3 to 8 weeks, depending on how many tools the work connects and how much content the assistant answers from.

How much does AI integration cost?

Every project is priced on a fixed written scope. Send a short brief and we reply within two working days with a price range, a timeline and what is included.

Do we own the AI accounts and the data?

Yes. You own your AI accounts and all data from day one. We hand over everything with documentation and training.
