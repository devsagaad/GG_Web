How we use AI

The rules every AI system we build follows, and what AI does on this website. Last updated 21 October 2026.

In the AI we build for clients

We build customer chatbots, workflow automation, internal assistants and agents that take action. Whatever the system, it is set up to these rules before it goes live:

  • It says what it is. An assistant tells the people it talks to that it is an assistant, not a person.
  • It answers from approved content. It draws only on content the client has approved, and hands difficult questions to a person.
  • A person reviews what matters. Anything sent to customers or published is reviewed by a person, and an agent asks a person to approve any action that matters.
  • Its limits are written down. What the AI may and may not do, including when it hands over to a person, is agreed with the client in writing before we build.
  • The data stays with the client. Data stays in the client's own accounts. The client owns the AI accounts and all data from day one.

On this website

The Ask Glint assistant on this site is a demonstration with pre-written answers. Nothing you click in it is sent to us or stored.

When we add an AI assistant that takes enquiries on this site, we will update this page and our privacy policy first, to say what it collects and how it is used.
