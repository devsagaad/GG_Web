How we use AI

The rules every AI system we build follows, and what AI does on this website. Last updated 22 October 2026.

In the AI we build for clients

We build systems with AI in them: booking and reminders, RSVP and shared galleries, site assistants, lead and inbox triage, and custom builds. Whatever the system, it is set up to these rules before it goes live:

  • It says what it is. An assistant tells the people it talks to that it is an assistant, not a person.
  • It answers from approved content. It draws only on content the client has approved, and hands difficult questions to a person.
  • A person reviews what matters. Anything sent to customers or published is approved by a person. An assistant that acts prepares the booking, the reply or the update, and a person confirms it.
  • Its limits are written down. What the AI may and may not do, including when it hands over to a person, is agreed with the client in writing before we build.

How data is handled

When we manage the AI for your systems, requests pass through our AI connection to the provider that runs the model. We store usage records (time, model, amount used), not the content of your conversations. We only keep conversation content if you ask us to while we troubleshoot something, and only for a short time. We'll tell you which AI providers process your data, and we sign a data processing agreement with every client. If you use your own AI account, your provider's terms apply to that account.

On this website

The Ask Glint assistant on this site is a demonstration with pre-written answers. Nothing you click in it is sent to us or stored.

When we add an AI assistant that takes enquiries on this site, we will update this page and our privacy policy first, to say what it collects and how it is used.
