FAQ

Before you ask

How long does a brand identity take?

A brand identity takes 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff. Combined with a website, the whole project usually runs about 12 weeks.

How much does a brand identity cost?

Every project is priced on a fixed written scope. Send a short brief and we reply within two working days with a price range, a timeline and what is included.

What is included in a brand identity?

A logomark and wordmark, a type and colour system, motion and 3D for the mark, and brand guidelines in Figma that your team can work from directly.

Do we own the logo and the files?

Yes. You own the design files and everything else we make for you from day one. We hand over everything with documentation.

Can you also build the website and the AI?

Yes. We are one studio for the identity, the Webflow website and the AI behind it, so they can run as a single project with one team and one written scope.
