FAQ

Before you ask

How long does a brand identity take?

A brand identity takes 4 to 6 weeks from kickoff. Combined with a website, the whole project usually runs about 12 weeks.

How much does a brand identity cost?

Every project is scoped to what you need, so there is no price list. Our assistant asks a few questions about your project and finds a time that works; then you meet the team, and a written proposal follows.

What is included in a brand identity?

A logomark and wordmark, a type and colour system, motion and 3D for the mark, and brand guidelines in a Brand Hub in your client portal that your team can work from directly.

Do we own the logo and the files?

Yes. You own the design files and everything else we make for you from day one. We hand over everything with documentation.

Can you also build the website and the systems?

Yes. We are one studio for the identity, the Webflow website and the systems behind them, AI included, so they can run as a single project with one written scope.
