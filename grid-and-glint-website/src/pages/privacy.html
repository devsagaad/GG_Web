Privacy policy

What we collect when you contact us, why, who else touches it, and how to ask us to change or delete it. Last updated 21 October 2026.

Who we are

Grid and Glint is a brand, web and AI studio in Quezon City, Metro Manila, Philippines. We are the personal information controller for information you send us through this website. You can reach us at info@gridandglint.com or +63 918 620 4713.

We handle personal information in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the rules of the National Privacy Commission.

What we collect

  • What you send us. Your name, your email address, the services you are interested in, and anything you write in a brief or an email.
  • Technical information. When you load a page, your browser sends your IP address and basic device information to the services that deliver the site: our host, and the services that serve our fonts and files. We do not use this to identify you.

We do not currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If we add them, for example to measure advertising, we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

The Ask Glint assistant on this site is a demonstration with pre-written answers. Nothing you click in it is sent to us or stored.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quote.
  • To plan and deliver a project if you become a client.
  • To keep the records the law requires us to keep.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for marketing you did not ask for.

Who else handles it

We use a small number of service providers to run this website and our email, including Webflow, which hosts the site and receives form submissions, and the services that deliver our fonts and files. Some of them are based outside the Philippines, so your information may be processed abroad. We remain responsible for it wherever it is processed.

How long we keep it

We keep an enquiry only as long as we need it to reply and follow up. If you become a client, we keep project records for the length of the project and for as long as the law requires afterwards. Then we delete them.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal information is used;
  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • object to its processing;
  • have it corrected if it is wrong;
  • have it erased or blocked;
  • receive a copy of it in a portable format;
  • claim damages for harm caused by its misuse;
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

To use any of these rights, email info@gridandglint.com. We reply within a reasonable time and may ask you to confirm who you are first.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top of the page. If the change affects how we use information you have already given us, we will tell you directly.
