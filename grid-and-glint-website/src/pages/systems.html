FAQ

Before you ask

What do you build?

Systems that take work off your plate: RSVP and shared galleries, booking and reminders, site assistants, lead and inbox triage, and custom builds we scope with you.

Is it safe for my customers and my data?

Assistants always say they're assistants, answer only from content you've approved, and hand hard questions to a person. A person approves anything that goes to a customer or gets published. We record usage, such as how many requests were made, not the content of your conversations.

Do I need my own AI account?

No. By default we manage the AI for you and keep it current. If you'd rather use your own account with an AI provider, we connect that instead and the provider bills you directly.

Who owns what?

You own your content, your data, and the system we build for you. When we manage the AI, we hold the provider accounts and you get your own key and usage dashboard. If you use your own AI account, that account is yours too.

How long does it take?

It depends on the build. Your Product Spec sets the timeline before any development starts.

How much does it cost?

Every build is scoped on a short call so you only pay for what you need. You get a written proposal after the call.
