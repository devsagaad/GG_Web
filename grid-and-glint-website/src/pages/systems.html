On-site

Yours, on your premises

The system runs on a server at your location. Your data stays on-site, the code is yours, and it keeps working on your local network when the internet is down.

THE LIMITS
  • You buy the server. We quote it separately and order it once it is paid.
  • Email, online payments and anything that calls an outside service wait until the connection is back.
  • Offline means inside your building's network, not on phones away from it.
  • Keeping it running takes a care plan, or fixes billed by the day.
AI

AI only where it helps, never on your server

AI is optional. If a system uses it, it calls an outside AI provider you approve. It never runs on your server, and we tell you exactly what leaves your premises. Prefer that nothing leaves? We build it without AI.

IF YOU USE AI
  • Assistants always say they are assistants.
  • They answer only from content you have approved.
  • A person approves anything that reaches a customer.
  • A monthly cap, with alerts before you reach it.
  • We record usage, not your conversations.
FAQ

Before you ask

What do you build?

One Custom System for each business, built around how it already runs. Examples: booking and reminders, staff records and internal tools, a site assistant, and lead and inbox triage.

Is it safe for my customers and my data?

Assistants always say they're assistants, answer only from content you've approved, and hand hard questions to a person. A person approves anything that goes to a customer or gets published. We record usage, such as how many requests were made, not the content of your conversations.

Does it need AI or the internet?

Neither. AI is optional, and when it is used it calls an outside provider you approve; it never runs on your server. The system itself runs on your local network, so it keeps working when the internet drops.

Who owns what?

You own your content, your data and the system we build for you, including the code, once the final payment clears. If the system uses AI, the provider account can be ours or yours.

How long does it take?

It depends on the build. Your Product Spec sets the timeline before any development starts.

How much does it cost?

A Custom System starts at ₱350,000, plus the server it runs on, which we quote separately. Your exact price is in the written proposal we send within 48 hours of our first call.

How does payment work?

50% when you sign, 30% when user testing starts and 20% before go-live. The server is paid in full before we order it. Code, data and admin access are handed over once the final payment clears.
