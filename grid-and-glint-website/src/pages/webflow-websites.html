FAQ

Before you ask

How long does a Webflow website take?

Most marketing websites take 6 to 10 weeks from kickoff to launch. A combined brand identity and website project usually runs about 12 weeks.

How much does a Webflow website cost?

Every project is priced on a fixed written scope. Send a short brief and we reply within two working days with a price range, a timeline and what is included.

Why do you build websites in Webflow?

Webflow gives your team a visual CMS to update pages without a developer, produces fast, clean pages that search engines read well, and hosts securely. We also build custom code when a project needs it.

Will the site be optimised for search and AI answers?

Yes. Every site launches with a clear heading structure, page titles and descriptions, structured data, fast load times, accessible markup and content written to answer real customer questions directly.

Can our team update the site ourselves?

Yes. Content lives in the Webflow CMS, and we train your team at handover and leave documentation, so changing a page does not need us.

Do we own the website?

Yes. You own the design files, the Webflow site, your domain and all data from day one. We hand over everything with documentation and training.
