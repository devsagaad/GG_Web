Umbrella Dental Clinic

A brand identity and Webflow website for a dental clinic in Quezon City that offers top-rated, affordable care and wants every visit to feel easy, never intimidating.

ServicesBrand identity, Webflow website
LocationQuezon City, Philippines
Year2025
Design decisions

Why it looks this way

A logo that works from a favicon to a sign

The mark is the clinic's name drawn literally: an umbrella, with a rainbow stripe across the canopy. It comes in three forms, the symbol alone, the name alone and the two locked together, so it fits a browser tab, a social avatar and a shop sign without being redrawn.

Rules keep it legible everywhere. The symbol is never shown narrower than 40 pixels on screen. The clear space on every side is the size of the letter C in the logotype. Over photos it appears only in white or black, never in colour.

Two typefaces with separate jobs

Subway Club, a display face with real character, is kept for the moments that need presence, such as a campaign line. TG Haido Grotesk does everything else: bold for headlines and buttons, regular for body copy.

Sizes step down in a fixed ratio. Headlines are four times the body size and subheads twice, and tracking tightens as type grows. Any designer, or any template, gets the same hierarchy without guessing.

A small core palette and strict pairings

Three core colours carry the brand: Umbrella Blue (#0F7AB5), Mint Green (#7DDF49) and white. A main palette of lighter and darker blues and three greys handles accents. An extended palette of warmer tones, including the red and yellow of the rainbow, is kept for illustration and campaigns.

Colours are flat fills only, with no gradients, and only approved pairings are allowed, so text always keeps its contrast.

Margins that scale with the format

Every layout takes its margins from its own size: the longest side divided by 25. Inside them, the space splits into three columns. Headline sizes move in steps of 8 points, subheads are half the headline and body copy half the subhead. A poster, a story and a web banner come out related without being copies of each other.

Photos that feel human, not clinical

The photo direction asks for candid moments in and around the clinic: relaxed patients, friendly staff and group selfies, in bright light, never stiff or posed. The guidelines sum up the promise as professional care that feels easy and reassuring: dentistry without pressure.

The website

The Webflow website is built on the same system, with the same type scale, palette and margins, so the clinic looks the same on its site as on its signs and posts.
